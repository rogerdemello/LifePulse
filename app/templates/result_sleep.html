Where that percentage comes from

{{ source_cycle }} is the US National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey: a real, public survey of the general population. The figure above is simply the share of respondents who gave your two answers and also reported snorting, gasping or stopping breathing in their sleep.

No model is involved. There is nothing fitted, nothing extrapolated, and nothing about you being outside a training range — it is a count. NHANES asks about symptoms rather than performing sleep studies, so this reflects what people report, not confirmed diagnoses.
