What this score is, and isn't

It is a checklist, not a prediction. The six factors and their weights are an editorial judgement informed by public guidance from the WHO, the UK Chief Medical Officers and sleep-medicine consensus — not fitted to outcome data. Someone else could weight them differently and be equally defensible.

Age is deliberately excluded: this measures what you can change. A high score does not mean you are well, and a low one does not mean you are ill.
