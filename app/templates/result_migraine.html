Sleep & Rest

  • Consistent schedule: Go to bed and wake at same time daily
  • 7-9 hours: Aim for optimal sleep duration
  • Dark, cool room: Optimize sleep environment
  • Wind-down routine: 30 min before bed, no screens
  • Stress management: Try meditation, deep breathing, or yoga

Nutrition & Hydration

  • Hydration goal: Drink 2-3 liters of water daily
  • Regular meals: Don't skip meals, especially breakfast
  • Caffeine caution: Limit to 1-2 cups, avoid after 2 PM
  • Avoid triggers: Aged cheese, processed meats, artificial sweeteners
  • Magnesium-rich: Leafy greens, nuts, whole grains

Physical Activity

  • Regular exercise: 30 min moderate activity, 5 days/week
  • Low-impact options: Walking, swimming, cycling, yoga
  • Warm-up gradually: Sudden intense exercise can trigger
  • Stay hydrated: Drink water before, during, after exercise
  • Listen to body: Stop if you feel migraine coming

Screen & Environment

  • 20-20-20 rule: Every 20 min, look 20 ft away for 20 sec
  • Blue light filter: Use on all devices, especially evening
  • Adjust brightness: Match screen to ambient light
  • Ergonomic setup: Proper posture, screen at eye level
  • Reduce glare: Use matte screens, adjust lighting

Track Your Triggers

Keep a migraine diary for at least 3 months. Record:

  • Date, time, and duration of each migraine
  • Foods eaten 24 hours before
  • Sleep quality and hours
  • Stress levels and activities
  • Menstrual cycle (if applicable)
  • Weather conditions
{% endif %}

When to See a Doctor

Consult a neurologist or headache specialist if you experience:

  • Sudden, severe headache ("thunderclap")
  • Headache with fever, stiff neck, or confusion
  • Headache after head injury
  • Chronic headaches worsening over time
  • Migraines occurring 15+ days per month
  • New neurological symptoms (vision changes, numbness)
  • Headaches disrupting daily life/work
  • Over-the-counter meds not helping
Treatment Options: A neurologist can prescribe preventive medications, acute treatments, or recommend specialized therapies like Botox injections or CGRP inhibitors for chronic migraines.
