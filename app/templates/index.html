What LifePulse is for

It’s for the moment before you decide whether something is worth seeing a doctor about. Answer some questions, see how your numbers compare with the population, and take the result with you.

Good for

  • Deciding whether to book an appointment
  • Working out what to ask when you get there
  • Seeing which lifestyle factors move your risk most

Not for

  • Diagnosing anything
  • Deciding not to seek care when you feel unwell
  • Replacing tests, examination, or your medical history

Nothing you enter is stored on our server

No account, no database, no trackers. Your answers are discarded the moment your result is rendered. If you choose to save a result for an appointment it is kept in this browser only.
